# Guest Access Accounts (GAP)

## Create An Account
You can create an account through the [Sponsor Services](https://guestaccount.princeton.edu)
You sign up with your personal email.  Your personal email will get an email to activate the account.

## Login To Describe
Use just your email (no @princeton.edu).  It takes a long time for the user to be created and show up in Entra.
